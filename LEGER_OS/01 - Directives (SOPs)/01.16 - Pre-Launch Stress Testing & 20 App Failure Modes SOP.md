## 1. Directive Goal & Purpose
Establish a continuous pre-launch and release qualification protocol that stress tests every new feature, architectural migration, and billing update against the **20 Deadliest App Failure Modes** before deploying to production.

---

## 2. The 5 Audit Pillars & Baseline Status

| Pillar | Focus Areas | System Status | Key Invariants |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |

---

## 3. Pre-Launch Action Items & Fix Checkpoints

1. **Error Boundaries:** Verify `src/app/error.tsx` and `src/app/global-error.tsx` exist and catch runtime React render exceptions.
2. **Edge Rate Limiting:** Apply rate limiters on public auth endpoints (`/login`, `/signup`, `/api/auth/*`) to block brute-force credential stuffing.
3. **In-App Feedback:** Maintain direct support and bug report submission mechanisms inside System Settings and the navigation drawer.
4. **Data Portability:** Ensure `/api/user/export` and `/api/user/erase` remain 100% operational on every schema migration.
