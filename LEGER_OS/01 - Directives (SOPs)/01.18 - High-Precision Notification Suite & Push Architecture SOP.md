# 01.18 - High-Precision Notification Suite & Push Architecture SOP

## 1. Overview & Objective
LEGER_OS implements a strictly empirical, zero-gamification, mathematical notification engine that dispatches timely Web Push alerts for actionable financial events across banking, subscriptions, budgeting, and portfolio tracking.

---

## 2. Supported Notification Channels

### 1. 💳 Instant Transaction Push & 1-Tap Merchant Resolver
* **Outflow (Expense)**:
  * Generic/Unknown: `{BankApp} · -€{Amount}` (e.g. `Santander · -€14.50`) $\rightarrow$ `Tap to identify store & categorize.`
  * Known Store: `{Merchant} · -€{Amount}` $\rightarrow$ `Transaction logged in LEGER_OS · Tap to view.`
* **Inflow (Income / Transfer / Refund)**:
  * Multi-lingual keywords (`entrada`, `crédito`, `recebida`, `reembolso`, `depósito`, `vencimento`, `salário`, `cashback`, `MB WAY recebido`, `enviou-te`) and AI semantic flow direction (`type: "inflow"`).
  * Push title: `{Sender/Bank} · +€{Amount}` (e.g. `MB WAY · +€25.00`).
  * Body: `Inflow received · Tap to identify sender & categorize.`
  * Capture Screen: Dynamic green highlight (`+€25.00`) with `"Captured Inflow / Income"` banner.

### 2. 💰 Payday & Cycle Reset Capture
* **Trigger**: Detects salary deposits (amount $\ge$ €500 or matching paycheck keywords like `DELOITTE`, `ORDENADO`, `SALARY`).
* **Title**: `Payday Logged · Cycle Reset`
* **Body**: `+€2,450.00 captured. New cycle initialized with €65.00/day spending budget.`
* **Action**: Directs to `/dashboard` with newly calibrated cycle baselines.

### 3. 🛡️ Subscription Pre-Charge Radar
* **Trigger**: 24-48 hours before an active recurring subscription or annual contract is expected to renew (evaluated against 30-day cadence history).
* **Title**: `Radar Alert · {MERCHANT} Due in {days}d`
* **Body**: `Upcoming charge of €17.99 detected on your active cycle timeline.`
* **Action**: Directs to `/radar` to review, pause, or cancel subscriptions.

### 4. ⚠️ Category Budget Threshold & Velocity Spike Alerts
* **Budget Limits (85% and 100%)**:
  * Fires when a category's cumulative monthly spend crosses 85% or exceeds 100% of its budget limit.
  * Title: `Budget Alert · {Category} at 85%` / `Budget Exceeded · {Category} at 104%`
  * Body: `€255.00 spent of €300.00 limit. Tap to review.`
* **Spending Velocity Spikes ($> 1.35\times$)**:
  * Fires when current cycle variable spending velocity surges $+35\%$ above historical baseline.
  * Title: `Velocity Alert · 1.38x Burn Rate`
  * Body: `Current cycle spending burn rate is trending +38% above normal baseline.`

### 5. 🌅 Daily Morning Financial Outlook Brief
* **Trigger**: 08:30 AM daily telemetry sweep.
* **Title**: `Morning Outlook · Day {d} of {totalDays}`
* **Body**: `Safe daily burn: €42.00 · Projected: +€310.00 · Velocity: 0.94x.`

### 6. 🏁 Cycle Closing & Surplus Wrap-Up
* **Trigger**: Final 48 hours of an active paycheck cycle.
* **Title**: `Cycle Closing · +€412.00 Surplus`
* **Body**: `You finished this cycle under budget. Tap to review savings & transfer surplus to investments.`

### 7. 📈 Portfolio Market Moves & All-Time Highs (PRO)
* **Single-Asset Movements ($\ge \pm 3.5\%$)**:
  * Triggered during server market sync cron (`/api/cron/sync-market-data`).
  * Title: `NVDA +4.8% · $132.40` or `BTC -4.2% · $61,200`
  * Body: `{Asset} moved +4.8% in the last 24h. Holdings value updated in Portfolio.`
* **Portfolio All-Time High Milestone**:
  * Fires when total investment valuation exceeds all previous recorded snapshot peaks by $\ge$ €100.
  * Title: `Portfolio Milestone · €25,000 ATH`
  * Body: `Your total investment portfolio reached a new all-time high of €25,000.`

## 3. Scalable 100k+ Timezone & Telemetry Architecture (Zero-Cost Blueprint)

1. **Timezone Partitioning (Hourly Cron Dispatcher)**:
   * Vercel Cron runs hourly at minute 30 (`30 * * * *`) hitting `/api/notifications/daily-outlook`.
   * User profiles maintain an auto-synchronized `timezone` (e.g. `Europe/Lisbon`, `America/New_York`, `Asia/Tokyo`, detected via `Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone`).
   * The cron only selects users whose local time is currently in the **08:30 AM** window, smoothing peak server load from 100k users to ~4,000 users per hour.

2. **Precomputed Telemetry Caching**:
   * Telemetry snapshots (`velocity`, `dailyVariableBurn`, `projectedSurplus`, `daysElapsed`, `netDelta`) are pre-baked to `profiles.cached_telemetry` on transaction writes and statement ingestions.
   * The notification workers read pre-computed numbers in a single indexed DB lookup with zero on-the-fly math overhead.

3. **Parallel Micro-Batch Fanout**:
   * Users in the active timezone window are processed in concurrent chunks of 25 (`Promise.allSettled`), completing full global batches in seconds well within serverless timeouts.

4. **Zero Marginal Messaging Cost ($0/mo)**:
   * RFC 8291 / 8292 Web Push dispatches route directly through Apple APNs / Google FCM servers with 0 per-message API fees.

## 4. Fast-Path Architecture & 0ms Hydration

1. **Sub-50ms Fast-Path**:
   * Tapping any transaction push notification (`/?resolveTxId={id}`) renders ONLY `<DedicatedPushResolver />`, bypassing heavy dashboard calculations, cycle charts, and Recharts rendering.
2. **AI Chat Auto-Scroll**:
   * Staggered multi-frame scroll trigger (`instant` + `smooth` at 320ms) ensures entering the assistant or switching sessions always focuses the latest message.
3. **0ms Synchronous Profile Hydration**:
   * `localStorage.getItem("leger_cached_profile")` eliminates free-tier lock flashing for PRO users.
