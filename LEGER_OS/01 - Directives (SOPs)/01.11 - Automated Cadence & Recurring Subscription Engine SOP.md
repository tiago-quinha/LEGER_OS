# 01.11 - Automated Cadence & Recurring Subscription Engine SOP

## Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the mathematical standards, multi-layer classification engine, and execution flow for automated transaction periodicity detection, fixed commitment isolation, silent price-hike alerts, and dedicated `/radar` interface management.

---

## 1. 4-Layer Cadence & Subscription Engine (`src/lib/cadence-detector.ts`)

1. **Layer 1 — Known Subscription Registry (Instant Match):**
   - 100+ global and Portuguese digital subscriptions, SaaS tools, telcos, utilities, gym chains, and streaming platforms (e.g. `NETFLIX`, `SPOTIFY`, `OPENAI`, `ANTHROPIC`, `CURSOR`, `EDP`, `VODAFONE`, `MEO`, `FITNESS HUT`, `APPLE`, `GOOGLE`, `ICLOUD`, `AMAZON PRIME`).
   - Detected on the very first occurrence ($N = 1$).

2. **Layer 2 — Direct Debit & Institutional Trigger Keywords:**
   - Detects debits featuring `SEPA`, `DEBITO DIRECTO`, `DD`, `MENSALIDADE`, `QUOTA`, `SEGURO`, `ANUIDADE`, or `PLANO`.

3. **Layer 3 — Interval Clustering & Variance Analysis:**
   - Groups recurring outflows with coefficient of variation $\sigma / \mu \le 0.40$:
     - **Monthly:** $\Delta t \in [25, 38]$ days.
     - **Annual:** $\Delta t \in [330, 380]$ days.

4. **Layer 4 — User Cadence Overrides & Custom Pinned Bills:**
   - Ingests `cadenceOverrides` stored in `localStorage` (`leger_subscription_cadence_overrides`).
   - Merges custom pinned recurring commitments (`leger_pinned_subscriptions`) created via the `+ PIN RECURRING BILL` drawer.

---

## 2. Invariants & Filtering Protocols

1. **Strict Cadence Normalization:**
   - Recurring subscriptions are strictly categorized as `"monthly"` or `"annual"`. Arbitrary or weekly frequencies are prohibited.

2. **100% CAPS LOCK Typography:**
   - All merchant titles, status badges, cadence pills, and search filters must be in 100% uppercase (`SPOTIFY`, `NETFLIX`, `MONTHLY`, `ANNUAL`, `PRICE JUMP`).

3. **Strict Recency Drop:**
   - Monthly items that have not billed in $>38$ days are dropped (inactive/cancelled).
   - Annual items that have not billed in $>380$ days are dropped.

4. **Financial Broker & Transfer Exclusion:**
   - Outflows to investment/brokerage accounts (`XTB`, `DEGIRO`, `TRADE REPUBLIC`, `BINANCE`, `KRAKEN`, `SAVINGS`, `TRANSFERENCIA`) are balance transfers and strictly excluded from subscription overhead.

5. **Normalized Price Jump Comparisons:**
   - Compares previous vs latest amounts only across like-for-like cadences.
   - If cadence shifted from monthly to annual, compares annualized equivalent rates to prevent false $+1700\%$ jump warnings.

6. **Anti-Gamification Invariant:**
   - No gamified financial health scores, trophies, or arbitrary XP badges. Calculations remain purely mathematical and factual.

## 3. Dedicated `/radar` Architecture

1. **Top-Level Route (`/radar`):**
   - Accessible from desktop sidebar and mobile bottom navigation dock.
   - Header follows normalized subpage standards: `<Radio className="h-3.5 w-3.5" /> SUBSCRIPTION RADAR` eyebrow + `RADAR` title in bold Geist Sans.

2. **Executive Metric Cards:**
   - `MONTHLY RECURRING` and `ANNUAL PROJECTED` cards in `<Tilt>` with auto-height compact text stacks.

3. **Silent Price Hike Banner:**
   - Amber alert banner (`bg-amber-500/10 border-amber-500/30`) displaying flagged rate jumps with old vs new amounts and percentage hike tags.

4. **Search Bar & Category Filter Tabs:**
   - Search: `h-8 pl-9 pr-3 text-xs bg-card border border-border/60 rounded-none font-sans`.
   - Filter Tabs: `px-3.5 py-1.5 text-[9px] font-mono font-bold uppercase tracking-wider` with active inverted `bg-foreground text-background`.

5. **Slide-Up Pin Drawer (`+ PIN RECURRING BILL`):**
   - Draggable bottom drawer allowing users to search historical statement debits or manually input any recurring overhead.
