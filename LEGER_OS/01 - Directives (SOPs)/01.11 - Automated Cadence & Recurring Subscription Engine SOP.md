# 01.11 - Automated Cadence & Recurring Subscription Engine SOP

## Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the mathematical standards and execution flow for automated transaction periodicity detection, fixed commitment isolation, and subscription price-hike alerts.

---

## 1. Cadence Detection Algorithm (`src/lib/cadence-detector.ts`)

1. **Merchant Normalization:**
   - Strips bank extract noise prefixes/suffixes (`MBWAY`, `PAGAMENTO`, `SIBS`, `COMPRA CC`, country/city suffixes).
   - Maps brand aliases (`SPOTIFY`, `NETFLIX`, `AMAZON PRIME`, `APPLE SERVICES`, `VODAFONE`, `EDP COMERCIAL`).

2. **Periodicity Classification:**
   - Evaluates inter-transaction delta ($\Delta t$) and coefficient of variation ($\sigma / \mu$):
     - **Weekly:** $\Delta t \in [5, 9]$ days.
     - **Bi-Weekly:** $\Delta t \in [12, 16]$ days.
     - **Monthly:** $\Delta t \in [25, 34]$ days (or same day-of-month $\pm 3$ days).
     - **Quarterly:** $\Delta t \in [80, 100]$ days.
     - **Annual:** $\Delta t \in [340, 380]$ days.

3. **Status Lifecycle:**
   - `active`: Charged within expected cycle window.
   - `overdue`: $\text{elapsed} > 1.5 \times \text{interval}$.
   - `cancelled`: $\text{elapsed} > 2.5 \times \text{interval}$.
   - `price_jump`: Latest amount $\ge 1.05 \times \text{baseline}$ with $\Delta \text{amount} \ge €0.50$.

---

## 2. Projection Engine Integration Invariant

1. **Fixed vs Variable Burn Separation:**
   - `simulateExpertDailyProjection` in both `src/components/DashboardView.tsx` and `src/lib/server-telemetry.ts` utilizes `detectRecurringCadence(...)` to identify fixed subscription commitments.
   - Fixed recurring debits are subtracted from current cycle outflow to derive clean variable burn.
   
2. **Mathematical Invariants Preserved:**
   - **Recency Decay ($\lambda = 0.12$):** Variable daily spending applies exponential decay weighting $w = e^{-\lambda \cdot \text{daysAgo}}$ (~6-day half-life).
   - **Current Cycle Alpha ($\alpha \ge 0.65$):** Weighting heavily favors active cycle performance:
     $$\alpha = \min\left(1.0, 0.65 + 0.35 \cdot \frac{\text{days elapsed}}{\text{total days}}\right)$$
   - Recurring merchants are scheduled on their expected day-of-month rather than smeared across daily variable burn.
